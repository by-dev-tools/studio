# Onward — project brief

Onward is invented. It exists so a fresh clone of Studio has something real to
look at, and so the shape of a project brief is visible rather than described.

Inherits the studio brief.

## What Onward is

A departure board for people already standing on a concourse. It answers one
question — *is my train going to be there, and when* — before you have finished
looking at it. It is not a journey planner and never becomes one.

## Non-negotiable

- **An unknown is never dressed as a number.** When the operator has told us
  nothing, the row says "No update". Inventing an estimate to avoid an awkward
  gap is the failure this product exists to avoid: a made-up number is trusted
  exactly as much as a real one, and it is wrong.
- **Status is never colour alone.** Every state carries its word. Colour is
  reinforcement, never the only channel — a boarding hall is bright, phones are
  glanced at, and plenty of people cannot separate the hues at all.
- **Cancelled is stated once, calmly.** Struck through, in the calm register.
  Nothing flashes; a cancelled train is not an emergency, it is information.
- **Never scold.** No "hurry", no countdowns designed to create urgency.
